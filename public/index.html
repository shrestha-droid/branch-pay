How it works

From brief to paid, hands-free.

  1. Scoper prices the request, under a hard cap enforced in code
  2. You approve, in full or with a 50% deposit
  3. Biller sends a real PayPal sandbox invoice
  4. Collector chases the payment on its own
  5. Builder delivers the moment PayPal says paid

Ledger every quote, invoice and payment

Agentic commerce

Let your client's AI hire you.
